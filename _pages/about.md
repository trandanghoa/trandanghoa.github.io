# Hoa Tran-Dang

Welcome to my academic website!

I am a **Research Professor** in the Department of IT Convergence Engineering at **Kumoh National Institute of Technology (KIT)**, Republic of Korea. I am also an **IEEE Senior Member**.

My research focuses on intelligent, distributed, and adaptive computing systems for next-generation networked environments. I am particularly interested in integrating artificial intelligence, communication technologies, and emerging computing paradigms to develop efficient and intelligent IoT and edge computing systems.

## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- **Internet of Things (IoT):** IoT architectures, intelligent sensing, and networked systems.
- **Edge and Fog Computing:** Distributed computing, computation offloading, resource allocation, and service management.
- **Distributed Artificial Intelligence:** Federated learning, multi-agent learning, and collaborative intelligence.
- **6G and Next-Generation Networks:** AI-enabled networking, intelligent resource management, and communication systems.
- **Digital Twin-enabled Computing:** Digital Twin-assisted optimization, adaptive learning, and distributed intelligence.
- **Quantum Computing and AI:** Quantum machine learning and quantum-enhanced optimization for intelligent computing.

## Research Activities

My research explores learning-based and optimization-driven approaches to address challenges in distributed computing and communication networks. I am particularly interested in developing adaptive algorithms that enable intelligent collaboration among distributed devices, edge servers, and network infrastructures.

My recent research investigates the integration of Digital Twins, distributed learning, and quantum-enhanced optimization to improve the efficiency, adaptability, and intelligence of future edge computing systems.
